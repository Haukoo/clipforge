Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current ClipForge personal developer project and its static information website. ClipForge is a local-first application operated on the user's computer, not a hosted media-processing service.

Local media and project data

Imported media, transcripts, captions, analysis, render revisions and project records are processed and stored locally. Media files remain local unless you explicitly choose to upload or publish them to a connected platform. You control local files and project deletion; copies you downloaded or backed up may remain until you remove them.

Connected platform accounts

You may authorize OAuth connections to supported platforms. ClipForge stores access and refresh tokens using its secure operating-system token store, currently Windows Credential Manager. Ordinary project database records contain non-secret account identity, capability information, token references and publication records rather than raw credentials.

Explicit publishing

When you explicitly confirm publishing, ClipForge may send the selected media, destination metadata and required account/API information to TikTok, YouTube or Instagram. Those services apply their own privacy policies, retention practices and account rules. ClipForge cannot guarantee deletion or retention behavior on a third-party platform after upload.

Diagnostics and this website

Local logs may contain non-secret diagnostic information, such as processing stages, project identifiers and redacted errors. ClipForge does not sell user data or use advertising trackers. This static website includes no analytics or advertising scripts. Its hosting provider may process standard requests, including IP addresses and browser information, under its own policies.

Disconnecting and revocation

You may disconnect accounts in ClipForge and revoke the application's access in the platform's connected-app settings. Local credential removal and platform-side revocation are distinct operations. Removing a local connection does not delete videos previously uploaded to a platform.

Contact and updates

For questions, contact the developer through the ClipForge repository. No separate project contact email or legal entity is designated. This policy may be updated as the application changes; the date above identifies this version.
