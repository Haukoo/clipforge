ClipForge is a local-first video editing and publishing workflow that helps users prepare short-form and vertical video content for supported social platforms.

Process locally

Import footage, analyze speech and scenes, and prepare vertical clips on your own computer.

Review every asset

Correct captions, review finished revisions and approve the exact video and destination metadata before publishing.

Connect when ready

TikTok, YouTube and Instagram integrations are available where configured and authorized. Platform availability and eligibility vary.

A workflow you control

Local media processing and exports work independently of connected accounts. Publishing requires your explicit review and a separate final confirmation.

ClipForge is an independent personal developer project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, YouTube or Instagram. It does not guarantee copyright clearance, monetization or platform acceptance.
